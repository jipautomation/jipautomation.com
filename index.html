How it works

Follow one document.
One of the five stages is a person.

Nothing reaches your books before that person clicks. Scroll, and watch the document change state on the right.

1 / 5

It arrives

A supplier emails a sales order. It lands in the folder your team already uses. Nobody uploads anything.

2 / 5

It gets read

Claude reads the PDF itself, scans included. Every header field and every line becomes a structured record.

3 / 5

It gets checked

Lines must add up to the printed total. Supplier and customer must match your master data. Anything off goes to an exceptions list, not into your books.

4 / 5

Someone approves it

Your reviewer gets one email with every field pre-filled. They fix what needs fixing, then approve. That click is the only way a row gets written.

5 / 5

It's filed

A linked database record, traceable back to the review that approved it. Downstream paperwork builds from that record, not from a spreadsheet.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Who is this for?+
Teams of two to twenty who retype vendor documents into spreadsheets and then hand-build paperwork from them: equipment and barrel financiers, lessors, wineries, importers, distributors, small lenders.
What if the AI reads a number wrong?+
It gets caught before it matters. Lines must sum to the printed total, and a person sees every field before anything is written. A document that fails a check goes to an exceptions list with the reason.
Do you need access to our systems?+
Read access to wherever the documents land (a shared folder or mailbox), and a place to write the results. The database is one I set up for you; you own the account.
What does it cost?+
A fixed price per phase to build, quoted after the demo, and a monthly plan to run it that includes the software subscriptions. No hourly billing on builds.
Who owns the workflows and the data?+
Your data is yours, exportable any time. You get a perpetual licence to your instance of the system and full documentation, so you could run it without me.
How long does a first slice take?+
Usually two to four weeks from the first call to a working demo on your real documents. The first client's first document type went end to end in about that.
