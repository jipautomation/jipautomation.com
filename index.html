How it works

Five building blocks.
One of them is a person.

Every automation I build is assembled from the same five parts, whatever the document or the industry. Scroll through them and the panel shows each one at work.

1 / 5

Intake

Documents arrive where they already do: a shared folder, a mailbox, an upload form. Each one is logged and classified before anything else happens, so nothing is lost and nothing is handled twice.

2 / 5

Extraction

Claude reads the document itself, scans and photos included. Header fields and every line item become structured data, with a confidence score on each field.

3 / 5

Checks

Totals must add up. Names must match your master data. Duplicates are caught. Anything that fails a check goes to an exceptions list with the reason, not into your books.

4 / 5

Approval

A person on your team sees every field before anything is written, pre-filled and one click from done. That click is the only way a record is created.

5 / 5

Output

The record files itself. The paperwork that used to be typed from it, notices, schedules, confirmations, signature packets, is generated from the record instead. A daily digest tells you it all ran.

FAQ

Fair questions.

Who is this for?+
Teams of two to twenty who retype documents from outside the business into spreadsheets or systems, then hand-build paperwork from the result: financiers and lessors, wineries and producers, importers and distributors, property managers, contractors, and professional services firms.
What if the AI reads a number wrong?+
It gets caught before it matters. Lines must sum to the printed total, names must match your master data, and a person sees every field before anything is written. A document that fails a check goes to an exceptions list with the reason.
Do you need access to our systems?+
Read access to wherever the documents land (a shared folder or mailbox), and a place to write the results. The database is one I set up for you; you own the account.
What does it cost?+
A fixed price per phase to build, quoted after the demo, and a monthly plan to run it that includes the software subscriptions. No hourly billing on builds.
Who owns the workflows and the data?+
Your data is yours, exportable any time. You get a perpetual licence to your instance of the system and full documentation, so you could run it without me.
How long does a first slice take?+
Usually two to four weeks from the first call to a working demo on your real documents. The first client's first document type went end to end in about that.
